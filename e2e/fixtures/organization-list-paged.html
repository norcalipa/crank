Company rankings

Compare companies by what matters to you, then open one to see the evidence.

How ranking works

Balanced

Weights every rating dimension evenly.

Company score
The preset-weighted average of the rating dimensions. It is the same for everyone.
Personal fit
Shown only in Job Search matches. It is computed from your saved priorities and is never mixed into the company score.
Rating coverage
How many of the active rating dimensions have at least one score for the company, shown as "N of M". A dimension with no score counts as missing, never as zero.
Fact coverage
How many of the 7 tracked facts (RTO policy, funding round, public status, accelerated vesting, locations, company name and company domain) have accepted evidence.
Freshness
"Last verified" is the most recent date a source was re-checked and still supported an accepted fact. Editing the company record does not re-verify facts. Each fact has its own re-check window:
  • RTO policy: 90 days
  • Funding round, public status and locations: 180 days
  • Accelerated vesting, company name and company domain: 365 days
Evidence status
  • ✓ Verified: accepted and re-checked within its window.
  • ! Stale: accepted, but not re-checked within its window.
  • ? Unknown: no accepted evidence yet.
  • • Profile data: a curated profile value without field-level evidence.
  • … Pending review: an observation is waiting for review and is not verified.
  • ⚠︎ Conflicting observation: a later observation disagrees and is not verified.
