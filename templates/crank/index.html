{{ algorithm.html_description_content |safe }}
Company score
The preset-weighted average of the rating dimensions. It is the same for everyone.
Fit
Shown only in Job Search matches. Computed from your saved priorities and never mixed into the company score.
Requirement coverage
Shown only in Job Search matches. The share of your requirements that could be decided either way. It does not mean the facts behind them are verified.
Rating coverage
How many of the active rating dimensions have at least one score for the company, shown as "N of M". A dimension with no score counts as missing, never as zero.
Fact coverage
How many of the {{ tracked_field_count }} tracked facts ({{ tracked_field_names }}) have accepted evidence.
Freshness
"Last verified" is the most recent date a source was re-checked and still supported an accepted fact. Editing the company record does not re-verify facts. Each fact has its own re-check window:
    {% for window in freshness_windows %}
  • {{ window.fields }}: {{ window.days }} days
    • {% endfor %}
Evidence status
  • ✓ Verified: Accepted and re-checked within its freshness window.
  • ! Stale: Accepted, but not re-verified within its freshness window.
  • ? Unknown: No accepted evidence for this fact.
  • • Profile data: Curated profile value without field-level evidence.
  • … Pending review: An observation is waiting for review. Not verified.
  • ⚠︎ Conflicting observation: A later observation disagrees. Not verified.
  • Sourced, not confirmed: An accepted, current source says this in its own words. Matching read it automatically and the reading has not been confirmed.
  • Evidence changed: The fact behind this result was replaced or removed after matches were computed. Refresh matches to re-check it.
